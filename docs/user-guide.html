Hand Gesture Control - User Guide

Control your Windows PC with your hand and a webcam. Everything runs on your PC - no video is saved or sent anywhere.
Part 1 · Getting started

1. Start the app

  1. Close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, Camera app).
  2. Double-click the Hand Gesture Control icon on your Desktop (blue circle with a white hand).
    No icon? Open C:\Users\SUHAIL KHAN\hand-gesture-control and double-click start.bat.
  3. Two windows open: a black console (shows a log of actions) and the camera preview. Keep both open.
  4. The app starts PAUSED. Hold an open palm 🖐️ for 1 second - it turns ACTIVE.

2. Where to put your hand

  • Sit 50-80 cm from the camera, palm facing it.
  • Keep your hand inside the yellow rectangle in the preview. That rectangle = your whole screen, so small moves are enough.
  • Good light on your hand helps a lot.
  • Your right hand controls; your left hand takes over if the right one is out of view. The hand in control is drawn blue, an ignored hand grey.

3. PAUSED and ACTIVE - what they mean

ModeWhat happens
PAUSEDThe app sees your hand but ignores it. Cursor does not move, nothing is clicked.
ACTIVEYour gestures control the PC.

It is a safety switch: pause it to scratch your head, drink water, or talk with your hands without clicking things.

The mode is shown in the top-left corner of the preview.

4. Switch between them

ToGestureKeyboard
PAUSEFist ✊ - hold 1 sCtrl+Alt+P
RESUMEOpen palm 🖐️ - hold 1 sCtrl+Alt+P

While you hold, a yellow progress bar fills on the right. Open your hand before it is full to cancel.

5. Why did it pause by itself?

  • It always starts paused.
  • Your hand rested in a loose fist for over a second - that counts as the pause gesture.
  • You pressed Ctrl+Alt+P.
Not using it for a moment? Just move your hand out of the camera view. No hand = nothing happens.

6. Stop the app

  • Ctrl+Alt+Q (works anywhere)
  • or Esc while the preview window is selected
  • or close the preview window with its X

Emergency stop: push your real mouse into any screen corner - the app stops immediately.

7. Helpers

  • Press H in the preview: list of all gestures.
  • start-dry-run.bat: practice mode, shows gestures but clicks nothing.
  • calibrate.bat: measures your hand so gestures fit you better.

Part 2 · Folders, scrolling, files

📁 Open a folder

  1. Point ☝️ (index finger up, others folded). Move your hand slowly - the cursor follows your fingertip.
  2. Stop with the cursor on the folder icon. Keep your hand still.
  3. Two quick pinches 🤏🤏: touch thumb + index tips, open, touch, open - as fast as a mouse double click.
  4. The folder opens. The preview shows LEFT CLICK then DOUBLE CLICK.
The cursor freezes while your fingers close, so it will not slide off the icon.
Other way: one pinch to select the folder, then thumbs up 👍 (hold 0.7 s) = Enter = open.

Only one click happened? Pinch faster.   It said DRAG? You held the pinch too long - make it a quick tap.

🗂️ Drag a file to another folder

  1. Point ☝️ the cursor onto the file.
  2. Pinch and keep holding 🤏 for about half a second - the preview shows DRAG.
  3. Still pinching, move your hand - the file moves with the cursor.
  4. Over the target folder, open your fingers - the file is dropped (DROP).
If your hand leaves the camera while dragging, the file is let go automatically.

↕️ Scroll up and down (folder, web page, document)

  1. Point ☝️ the cursor onto the area you want to scroll (the file list, the web page). Windows scrolls whatever is under the cursor.
  2. Make the V sign ✌️ (index + middle up) and hold still a moment - this height becomes your starting point.
  3. Move your hand up or down:
You doResult
Hand a little up from the start pointScrolls up slowly
Hand further upScrolls up faster
Hand down from the start pointScrolls down (further = faster)
Hand back to the start pointScrolling stops
Change gesture (e.g. back to ☝️)Scrolling stops completely
It works like a joystick: hold your hand above the start point and it keeps scrolling. Tiny shakes near the start point do nothing.

📋 Right-click menu

  1. Point ☝️ at the item (file, desktop, text).
  2. Touch thumb + middle finger tips 👌 - the menu opens.
  3. Point at a menu entry and pinch once 🤏 to choose it.

📌 Example: open Documents and scroll down

🖐️ hold 1 s → ACTIVE  →  ☝️ cursor on Documents  →  🤏🤏 opens  →  ☝️ cursor on the file list  →  ✌️ hold, then lower hand = scroll down  →  raise back = stop  →  ✊ hold 1 s = PAUSED

Part 3 · Beyond the mouse

🌍 Google Earth Pro / maps / 3D

  • Zoom: three fingers 🖖, move hand up = zoom in, down = zoom out.
  • Zoom like VR: pinch with both hands 🙌, pull apart = in, push together = out.
  • Move the map: pinch & hold 🤏 and move (drags the globe).
  • Tilt / rotate: four fingers 🤚 (thumb folded), move your hand.
The app notices Google Earth and Blender automatically and uses the right zoom for them.

🔎 Zoom in a browser, Word, photos

Three fingers 🖖 up/down = Ctrl + mouse wheel zoom. Put the cursor over the page first.

🎵 Music, video & volume

  • Volume: call-me sign 🤙 (thumb + pinky out), move hand up = louder, down = quieter.
  • Play / Pause: rock sign 🤘 (index + pinky up), hold 0.6 s.

👋 Swipes (open palm, quick flick)

SwipeNormallyIn a browser
←Switch appBack
→Switch appForward
↑Task viewTask view
↓Show desktopShow desktop

In PowerPoint: ← / → = previous / next slide. A still palm never swipes.

⌨️ Keys & shortcuts (hold the gesture)

  • Thumbs up 👍 0.7 s = Enter
  • Thumbs down 👎 0.8 s = on-screen keyboard
  • Pinky only 0.8 s = screenshot (Win+Shift+S)

🔒 Privacy

The camera image is processed on your PC and thrown away right after. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

Tips for best results

  • Sit 50-80 cm from the camera, palm facing it.
  • Keep your hand inside the yellow rectangle - it covers the whole screen, no need to reach the edges.
  • Good, even light on your hand; avoid a bright window behind you.
  • A plain background helps tracking.
  • Right hand is in control; your left hand takes over when the right one is out of view. The hand in control is drawn blue, an ignored hand grey.
  • Finger names on screen turn green when that finger counts as "up".
  • Make gestures clearly and hold them still for a moment - each gesture needs a few steady frames.

Reading the preview window

  • ACTIVE / PAUSED / DRY RUN - top left
  • Gesture: what it sees now · FPS top right (25+ is good)
  • Yellow text - the action just done (e.g. LEFT CLICK)
  • Progress bar - a hold gesture charging up
  • Bottom lines - fingers up, pinch distance, which hand

Something feels off?

Clicks become drags → pinch quicker, or raise timing.drag_hold_s.
Gestures trigger too easily / too hard → run calibrate.bat.
Cursor shaky / slow → tune smoothing in config.json.
Turn a gesture off → set it to null in gesture_actions.
